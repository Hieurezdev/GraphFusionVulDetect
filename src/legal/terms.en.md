# Terms of use and software licence

## 1. The service

ScanGuard analyses the source code of Solidity smart contracts to find signs of timestamp dependency and reentrancy vulnerabilities, explains each alert and helps fix it.

## 2. Limits of the results

- Results are produced by machine-learning models and a static analysis tool. They are alerts for an expert to confirm, not a verdict on how safe a contract is.
- The absence of alerts does **not** mean the contract is safe. The service does not replace a full security audit.
- A fix suggested by the assistant or the repair agent must be approved by an expert and tested with the project's own test suite before it is deployed.
